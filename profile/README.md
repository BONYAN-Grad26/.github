**Bonyaan** is an integrated, AI-powered lifestyle assistant designed to help individuals maintain a healthy, balanced lifestyle with ease. This organization hosts the repositories for the Bonyaan ecosystem, spanning cross-platform mobile and web applications, robust backend services, and advanced AI computer-vision microservices.

## 🌟 Our Mission

Maintaining a healthy lifestyle shouldn't feel like a second job. Bonyaan unifies personal wellness by eliminating the fragmentation of scattered fitness apps, static diet plans, and confusing workout routines. By leveraging Artificial Intelligence, Bonyaan adapts to your personal goals, dietary preferences, and fitness levels to provide dynamic, real-time guidance exactly when you need it.

## 🛠️ The Ecosystem

Bonyaan is built using a modern, scalable microservice architecture. Here are the core repositories that power our system:

### 📱 [Bonyan-Mobile-App](https://github.com/Organization-Name/Bonyan-Mobile-App)
The primary user interface built with **Flutter**. It delivers a seamless native experience for managing health profiles, viewing AI-generated meal and workout plans, and interacting with our smart features like the gym machine scanner and AI chatbot.

### 🌐 [Bonyan-Frontend](https://github.com/Organization-Name/Bonyan-Frontend)
A responsive web application built with **Next.js**, **React**, and **TailwindCSS**. It provides web access to Bonyaan's core features with an intuitive, accessible, and fast UI.

### ⚙️ [MetriPlan-API](https://github.com/Organization-Name/MetriPlan-API)
The central backend service built with **Java Spring Boot**. It handles user authentication (JWT), database management (MySQL), and acts as the main gateway orchestrating requests between the client frontends and our AI microservices.

### 🧠 AI & Machine Learning Services
We employ specialized Python microservices to power Bonyaan's intelligent features:
- **[Meal_Suggest](https://github.com/Organization-Name/Meal_Suggest):** A **FastAPI** service featuring computer vision. It analyzes photos of raw ingredients (like beef and potatoes) and instantly suggests healthy recipes and tailored meals.
- **[Bonyan-Chatbot](https://github.com/Organization-Name/Bonyan-Chatbot):** A conversational NLP assistant that provides dietary advice, modifies workout/meal plans on the fly, and answers health-related queries in multiple languages (including Arabic and English).

## ✨ Key Features

- 🥗 **Intelligent Meal Planning:** Dynamically generates personalized weekly meal plans based on calories, macros, and dietary restrictions (e.g., Vegan, Keto).
- 🏋️ **Adaptive Workout Planner:** Produces structured weekly training splits tailored to your experience level, targets, and available equipment.
- 📸 **Snap & Discover:** Take a picture of gym equipment to learn how to use it safely, or snap a photo of ingredients in your fridge to get a customized AI recipe.
- 💬 **AI Lifestyle Chatbot:** An intelligent assistant ready to answer your fitness questions and adjust your plans seamlessly via chat.

## 💡 Behind Bonyaan

Bonyaan was built from the ground up to bridge the gap between complex health science and everyday usability. What started as a comprehensive capstone engineering project at **Mansoura University** has evolved into a full-stack ecosystem. 

By integrating mobile development, web technologies, backend microservices, and AI models into one unified platform, we set out to prove that managing personal health can be accessible, intelligent, and seamless.
